# I Want To Listen To

Music Assistant plays from music sources, and there are a lot of them. Rather than reading
through the list one at a time, start from what you actually want to hear and see which sources
provide it.

Pick a heading below to jump to the sources for it. Most sources appear more than once, because
plenty of them do several jobs at the same time.

<ListenTo />

## Comparing sources side by side

Every source above has its own page covering what it can do, what it costs and how to set it up.
To weigh two of them against each other instead, the table below summarises all of them at once.
Green ticks mark a desirable quality or function.

Stream quality is shown as either [Hi-Res](/player-support/#audio-quality), <a href="https://www.soundguys.com/high-bitrate-audio-is-overkill-cd-quality-is-still-great-16518/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">CD quality</a> or lossy, with the codec and bitrate where available.

The most user friendly login method is a password. Next best is OAuth, where you log in on the service's own website and grant Music Assistant access to your account. The cookie method, where you copy a hidden value out of your browser, is the least desirable.
