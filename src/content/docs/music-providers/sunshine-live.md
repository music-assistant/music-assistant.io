# Sunshine Live <img src="/assets/icons/sunshine-live-icon.png" alt="Sunshine Live logo" style="width: 70px; float: right;" loading="lazy" />

Music Assistant has support for <a href="https://www.sunshine-live.de" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Sunshine Live</a>. Contributed and maintained by <a href="https://github.com/jordiboehme" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">jordiboehme</a>

Sunshine Live is a German radio station for electronic music, from Mannheim. Next to its main program it has channels for single styles and decades, like Techno, Trance, House and the 90s, and for festivals like Mayday and Nature One.

This source adds all channels to Music Assistant as radio stations, in the best quality the station offers.

## Configuration

In the configuration, you only need to click the save button.

## Usage

Browse the channels at *Browse > Sunshine Live*, or search for them by name. Channels can be added to your library from the station menu.

The title and artwork of the current song show a few seconds after a channel starts. During a live show you see the name of the show, which can take a minute or two to change.

## Known Issues / Notes

- The artwork comes from the station when it has a cover for the song, otherwise from a lookup by artist and title. It can be missing or wrong for rare tracks and long live sets.
- A seasonal channel like Xmasbeats can show only its name at times.
