# VRT MAX <img src="/assets/icons/vrt_max-icon.svg" alt="Preview image" style="width: 70px; float: right;" loading="lazy" />

Music Assistant has support for [VRT MAX](https://www.vrt.be/vrtmax/), the streaming platform of the Flemish public broadcaster (VRT). Contributed and maintained by [bollewolle](https://github.com/bollewolle)

With VRT MAX you can stream the VRT radio stations live (Radio 1, Radio 2, Klara, Studio Brussel, MNM, VRT NWS and the digital-only channels), listen back to past radio broadcasts, and play the official podcasts. Live radio is free and needs no account; on-demand playback, your favourites and resume-where-you-left-off require a free VRT account. Some on-demand content may only be available to listeners in Belgium.

### Other

- Live radio stations play without an account. On-demand playback, library favourites and resume position require a (free) VRT account.
- Radio listen-back episodes expose the songs played during the show as chapters, visible in the Now Playing view.
- Your VRT "Mijn lijst" favourites are synced to the Music Assistant library as podcasts, and adding or removing a podcast in Music Assistant is written back to VRT.
- Playback progress is read from and written back to your VRT account, so you can continue an episode across devices.
- The provider supports multiple instances, so each member of a household can connect their own VRT account.

## Usage

Add the provider from Settings → Music Sources. Leave the username and password empty to use live radio only, or enter your VRT account credentials to unlock on-demand playback, favourites and resume.

To connect more than one VRT account, add the provider again as a separate instance.

## Settings

- <b>Username.</b> The email address of your VRT account. Leave empty for live radio only.
- <b>Password.</b> The password of your VRT account. Leave empty for live radio only.

## Known Issues / Notes

- Some on-demand programmes and podcasts may be geo-restricted to Belgium.
- Station logos and programme metadata are populated from VRT where available.
