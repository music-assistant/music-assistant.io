# FIP <img src="/assets/icons/fip-icon.svg" alt="Preview image" style="width: 70px; float: right;"  loading="lazy" />

Music Assistant has support for <a href="https://www.radiofrance.fr/fip" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">FIP</a> which allows easy addition of its radio stations to Music Assistant. Contributed and maintained by <a href="https://github.com/culmat" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Matthias</a>

FIP is the music station of Radio France, the French public broadcaster, known for its eclectic mix of jazz, rock, world, electronic and chanson without advertising. Alongside the main station it runs twelve webradios, each dedicated to one style, from FIP Jazz and FIP Groove to FIP Metal and FIP Sacré français !

This source adds FIP and its twelve webradios to Music Assistant as radio stations. You would use this provider over another if you want the title, artist and cover art of each song, and the name and picture of the programme during FIP's live shows. FIP's own streams carry no song information.

### Stations

FIP, FIP Rock, FIP Jazz, FIP Groove, FIP Monde, FIP Nouveautés, FIP Reggae, FIP Electro, FIP Metal, FIP Pop, FIP Hip-Hop, FIP Sacré français ! and FIP Cultes.

## Configuration

In the configuration, you only need to click the save button

## Usage

Use the browse view or search to find stations to add to the library.

While a station plays, the player shows the song or programme on air with its cover. The browse view and a station's page show the station itself.

## Known Issues / Notes

- The song details usually change with the music, but now and then up to 40 seconds after the next song has started
- When a station has no song or programme to report, the station name and picture are shown instead
